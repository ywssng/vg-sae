## Exact next research decision

The root continuation proposes a narrowly scoped finite-noise certificate:
combine the explicit uniform approximation bound in prelimit proof equation (11)
with a certified limiting objective margin against q outside a declared interior
bracket. If successful, this replaces an unevaluated “sufficiently small noise”
quantifier by an explicit positive-noise instance. It must bound the complete
excluded parameter set and account for unconstrained fitted variance, rather than
only refine the location of a selected stationary root. It is substantive theory
closure for the normalized supplied two-atom model, not native-q success, a new
estimator, or completion of the overall pre-LLM objective.

After that bounded closure, the original native route is the exact unresolved
target stated in `AVERAGE_RISK_MARGIN_BRIDGE_20261001.md` §5: establish or refute a
population free-energy gap outside an epsilon-neighborhood of the positive
permutation teacher orbit at fixed finite beta/gamma in the actual sigmoid/
softplus amortized model. A meaningful counterexample must give representable
native parameter tensors, including bias and amplitudes. A freely optimized
per-input oracle lower envelope alone cannot refute the amortized class.

A bounded Colab CPU follow-up can inspect analytically chosen K=d=2 competitors
(recovering, complemented/biased, mixed and dense), using deterministic Gaussian
quadrature and an explicit tail/roundoff error budget. Freeze finite beta/gamma,
noise, conditioning/bias domain and geometry distance before observing values.
No training, new gamma search, new source selection or confirmation access is
required. A gap witness would turn an optimization bound into the conditional
recovery premises; a valid lower-energy counterexample would identify the precise
restriction needed for a future method version. Either result must still be
connected separately to the hard-between selection law before reopening W2.
